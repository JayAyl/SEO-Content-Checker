# SEO rules

## Title tag
- 30 to 60 characters including the brand suffix. Aim for under 580px.
- Format: `[Primary keyword phrase] | Volvo Cars`
- Primary keyword near the start. One keyword focus per title.
- Each title unique across the site. No ALL CAPS, no keyword lists, no emoji.

## Meta description
- 120 to 155 characters.
- Include the primary keyword once, naturally.
- One clear benefit and one call to action that matches the brief's `cta`.
- No double quotes (they can break the HTML attribute). No emoji.

## Page content
- Match the search intent in the brief.
- Primary keyword in the H1 and in the first 100 words. Secondary keywords in H2s where natural.
- Answer the main question early (helps AI answer engines and featured snippets).
- Short paragraphs (2 to 4 sentences). Use lists for specs or steps.
- Add internal links from the brief's `internal_links` with descriptive anchor text, never "click here".
- Include an FAQ section when the brief lists `faqs`.
