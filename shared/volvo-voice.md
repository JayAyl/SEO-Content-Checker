# Volvo Cars voice and naming

> Replace the sections marked PLACEHOLDER with the official Volvo Cars brand and tone guidelines.

## Brand naming
- Use "Volvo Cars" as the brand in title tags. Use "Volvo" in body copy where natural.
- Write model names exactly as Volvo does: EX30, EX40, EC40, EX90, ES90, XC40, XC60, XC90, V60, V90. No spaces, no hyphens.
- Powertrain terms: "fully electric", "plug-in hybrid", "mild hybrid". Do not say "EV" in titles unless the brief's keyword uses it.
- Never write "Volvo's" as a plural or misspell model names.

## Tone (PLACEHOLDER)
- Calm, confident, human. Scandinavian simplicity: short sentences, no hype.
- Lead with the benefit to the person, then the feature.
- No superlatives ("best", "safest", "most advanced") unless the brief gives a sourced claim.

## Claims
- Range, charging times, prices, emissions, safety ratings and awards must come from the brief, with the source noted.
- If a fact is missing, write `<!-- TODO: confirm [fact] -->` and continue.

## Market and spelling
- Follow the `locale` field in the brief (en-GB = British spelling, en-US = American spelling).
- Use market units (miles for UK and US, km elsewhere) and market currency.
